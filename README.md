# AI's Water Bill

A conservative, audited analysis of how much water AI and data centers actually use in the United States — per prompt, per person, and nationally — plus where the water really comes from, where the local harm concentrates, and what a site-specific water score would require.

Single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

**Live:** https://go-uda.github.io/ai-water-bill/

## What it covers

- Per-prompt and per-person footprints at a deliberately conservative setting
- The direct/indirect split: roughly five sixths of the footprint is consumed at power stations, not data centers — and how cooling technology can invert that
- National scale against the honest denominator (consumption, not withdrawal)
- Where the local harm actually concentrates, with a state-level water-stress map
- Whether large-scale buildout in water-abundant regions is feasible
- Federal, state and local policy levers
- A methodology appendix on site-specific water scoring, and a visible corrections log

## Method

Fifteen parallel research passes, roughly 700 tool calls, with arithmetic recomputed rather than inherited and primary sources opened where figures were load-bearing. The methods section in the page records what was corrected — including three figures revised against the report's own argument — and where the evidence remains weak.
